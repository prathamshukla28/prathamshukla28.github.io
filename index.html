I'm a data and machine-learning engineer with around five years of hands-on experience taking models past the notebook and into services people can actually call. I care about the unglamorous parts that make ML reliable: leak-free pipelines, reproducible training, tests, and clean deployment.

Flagship project

End-to-end ML service

ChurnGuard — Customer Churn Prediction

A service that predicts which telecom customers are about to cancel, so a retention team can reach them first. It covers the full lifecycle: data → features → model selection → evaluation → a served REST API, with the engineering practices of a production system.

  • ✓Leak-free pipeline. All scaling and encoding live inside a scikit-learn Pipeline, fit only on the training fold and cross-validated as one unit.
  • ✓Automatic model selection. Logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting are compared by 5-fold CV ROC-AUC; the winner is saved.
  • ✓Served with FastAPI. /predict returns a churn probability and a risk band; a new, month-to-month fibre customer scores 0.97, a loyal two-year customer 0.01.
  • ✓Reproducible & shipped. One seed through the whole run, a pytest suite, a Dockerfile that trains into the image, and GitHub Actions CI on Python 3.10 & 3.12.
